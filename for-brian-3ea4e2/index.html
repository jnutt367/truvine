For Brian

A letter from your brother

The Film

The letter, read aloud

Teddy reads it, the way Jason asked him to.

About four minutes. The words are written on the film itself, as it plays.

Brother, I know you’ve had a long spiritual journey of your own, and I’ve always respected how honestly you’ve walked it.

I’m not writing to change your mind about any of it.

I just realized something: you’ve never actually seen what I’ve been doing — what the Lord has been building through me these past couple of years, or what your own drawing has been carrying.

I want to show you. That’s all.

Come and see — and the door stays open either way.

— Jason

The Keys

Brian’s Keys

These are keys, Brian — not doors anyone will push you through. Keep them in your pocket. Any one of them, any order, any day — or none at all. They’ll keep.

  • Word of God Risen

    The whole Bible, read out loud by Teddy — every chapter free. Your drawing is the first thing it shows you.

  • Come and Hear

    Jason’s story, in his own words, told plainly — what God has done.

  • The Mark Film

    The first chapter of Mark, drawn frame by frame and told slow.

  • The Reach Poster

    One picture of everywhere your drawing has been — with today’s numbers standing beneath it. Tap to see it as it stands today.

  • Start Here

    The porch of the whole farm — every door, in one calm place.

  • The Field Report

    The plain record — who’s hearing, and where. Nothing dressed up.

  • The Store

    The little store — the emblem you designed, on real goods. Opened October 15, 2026.
