The graph — one column a week

Each column is the whole audience for that snapshot, stacked to 100%: the biggest nations named, the smaller nations gathered into one band, and the share YouTube couldn't place in any country drawn pale and hatched at the foot — it isn't a place. A new column joins every week, so the bands can be watched as they grow.

    Read these numbers honestly: the percentages are shares of all views, exactly as YouTube Studio reports them. YouTube could not place 46% of this month's views in any country — those views simply aren't in its table, and no country here gets credited with them. On the graph above, that unplaced share is the pale hatched band at the foot of the column — not a country. Of the views YouTube could place, the United States is 38.2% and the Philippines is 27.1% — America's viewers also stay the longest, at 39.6% of all watch time.
