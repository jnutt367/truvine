A Welcome Note

Welcome, friend — I’m Jason.

I’m just a regular fellow who loves Jesus, and I built TruVINE with Grandpa Teddy because I wanted the Word of God to be easy to hear and easy to share. Teddy has read the whole Bible out loud, every chapter, and we put it in a free app called Word of God Risen. There is no account to make, no password to remember, and nothing to pay.

You tap a book, tap a chapter, and tap Listen, and Teddy reads to you. If you get lost, tap Teddy’s face in the app and he’ll show you around, slow and plain. We’re glad you’re here, whether you stay for one chapter or pull up a chair for a while. Take your time — the door is open.

— Jason

In Plain Words

What We’re Doing

Maybe a friend sent you here, or maybe you found one of the films first. Either way, here is the whole farm in one place: TruVINE is a small ministry with a big Bible at its heart.

At the center is Grandpa Teddy, the kind old farmer who does the telling around here. He has read the whole Bible out loud — every single chapter — and it all lives in the free Word of God Risen app, so anybody can open it and listen.

Those same chapters are becoming animated films on YouTube, word for word, starting with the Gospel of Mark. There is also a Family Wall where folks are welcomed by hand, a Field Report that follows the Word as it goes out, and a little store for folks who want to carry the Word with them.

Everything here is free. There is no account to make and nothing you have to figure out before you begin.
