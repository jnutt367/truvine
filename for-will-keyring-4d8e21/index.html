    The Fight Behind It — What We’re Working Through

    Jason’s told you some of this at the counter, Will. Here it is in one place — plain and honest, the struggle told the same way as the fruit.

    Teddy’s voice — the chapters being rebuilt

    Every chapter in the app is checked word for word against the Bible text. This fall, some of Grandpa Teddy’s recordings were found coming out wrong — garbled in places, looping, words missing. So Jason set a rule that doesn’t bend: the moment a chapter is flagged, it comes down first and gets checked after. Nobody hears God’s Word wrong on our watch — “God is not the God of confusion, but of order” (1 Corinthians 14:33).

    Right now 453 of the 1,520 chapters are down and being rebuilt clean, one at a time — and each one has to pass the word-for-word check before it goes back up. Nine are already fixed and back. The 1,067 chapters still up are verified clean.

    The lights — when the counting went dark

    You know the little lights on the Earth map — one blooms when a new place opens the Word. For a stretch, the counting behind them went dark: the storage it lived in, on Vercel where the app runs, overflowed its limits. The readers never stopped reading — only the tally went out.

    The counting has since moved to a new home, and the lights are back on — every place that has lit since is counted again. California lit today, and it was counted.

    Pray with us, Will — that Teddy’s voice comes back clean, and every one of those chapters finds its way home. You’re a friend of this work; Jason wanted you to know the fight as well as the fruit.
