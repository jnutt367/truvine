Privacy Policy — TruVINE Captions

Effective November 22, 2026 · Operated by Jason Nutt (TruVINE)

TruVINE Captions is a small, private tool used by one person — the owner of the TruVINE YouTube channel — to upload caption and subtitle files to his own YouTube videos through the official YouTube Data API. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's own Google authorization (OAuth), the tool can add caption tracks to videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. That is the only thing it does. It does not read, collect, or store information about viewers, subscribers, or any other person.

What is stored

The only thing kept is the owner's own OAuth authorization token, stored locally on the owner's own computer so captions can be uploaded without signing in every time. No Google user data is copied to any server, database, or third party. Caption files themselves are the owner's own subtitle files for his own videos.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to anyone. TruVINE Captions' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account under Third-party access, and the locally stored token can be deleted at any time, which ends the tool's access completely.
