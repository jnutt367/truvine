The Note

Hey Ralph —

You, me, and your wife, playing trivia every single day. And let’s be honest about how it goes: you’re a tough out, Ralph. Most days the two of us split it right down the middle, and you keep me honest. I figured if anybody could handle one more question from me, it’s you.

Today’s Veterans Day. Of the two of us, I’m the one who wore the uniform — and on a day like this, it felt right to finally share something with an old friend that’s been on my heart for a while now.

Here’s the honest part: I don’t know if I’ve ever actually shown you this, or even mentioned it. I didn’t want to be weird about it. It’s just something I’ve been building that matters a lot to me, and you’ve been my friend since we were both in our twenties — so here it is.

It’s called Word of God Risen. It’s a free app with the whole Bible, read out loud, word for word, by a narrator we call Grandpa Teddy. No account, no password, nothing to sign up for. You just open it, tap a book, tap a chapter, and tap Listen.

And here’s why I’m really sending it to you, of all people: you’re sharp, and you’ll tell me the truth. Would you test-drive it for me? Open it up, tap the book of Mark, chapter 1, and hit Listen — or watch one of the short videos. Then tell me honestly: was it easy to find your way around, or was it confusing? Don’t be polite. Your straight answer is the favor, and it would mean a lot.

And if it’s not your thing, that’s fine too. No pressure, no follow-up campaign — we’re still on for trivia tomorrow either way.

— Jason
