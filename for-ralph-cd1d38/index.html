The Note

Hey Ralph —

You, me, and your wife. Trivia. Every day. You’re a tough out, Ralph — that’s the official position, and I’m not taking questions.

It’s Veterans Day, and of the two of us I’m the one who wore the uniform — so I get exactly one sentimental paragraph. This is that paragraph. Savor it; they don’t come often.

The honest part: I don’t know if I’ve ever actually shown you this. I didn’t want to be weird about it. Consider this me being weird about it — briefly, and only once.

It’s called Word of God Risen — a Bible app I built. The whole Bible, read out loud. Free, no account, no password.

The favor: test-drive it. Tap Mark, chapter 1, hit Listen — then tell me straight whether finding your way around was easy or a maze. Don’t be polite. You’ve never once been polite about my trivia answers; don’t start now.

And if it’s not your thing, it’s not your thing. No pressure, no follow-up, no newsletter with your name on it. Trivia tomorrow, same time.

— Jason
