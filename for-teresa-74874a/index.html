Put the App on Your Home Screen

Do this once, and the Bible is one tap away forever after — no typing, ever again.

If your phone is an Android

  1. Open the Bible with the gold button above (it opens in Chrome).
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right.
  3. Tap “Install app,” then tap “Install.”

Done — the emblem is on your home screen, named “Word of God.”

If your phone is an iPhone

  1. Open the Bible with the gold button above (it opens in Safari).
  2. Tap Share — the square with the arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll and tap “Add to Home Screen,” then tap “Add.”

Done — the emblem is on your home screen, named “Word of God.”
