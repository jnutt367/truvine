TruVINE

TruVINE is Jason Nutt’s personal channel-management tool for the TruVINE YouTube channel, @TruVINE365.

What this app does

The channel owner uses TruVINE with the YouTube Data API to operate and maintain his own channel. Depending on the task the owner authorizes, the tool can:

  • Read channel and video statistics for ministry reporting.
  • Review channel and video metadata, including titles and descriptions.
  • Prepare and apply updates to the owner’s own channel content, including metadata and descriptions.
  • Assist with authorized uploads and thumbnail work for the owner’s channel.
  • Assist with comment-related review and channel-maintenance tasks.

Who uses it

TruVINE is a private, owner-operated tool. It is not offered as a public sign-in service, and it operates only on the Google and YouTube account that the channel owner authorizes.

Privacy

Google user data accessed through the YouTube Data API is used only to operate and maintain Jason Nutt’s own TruVINE channel. It is not sold and is not used for advertising.
